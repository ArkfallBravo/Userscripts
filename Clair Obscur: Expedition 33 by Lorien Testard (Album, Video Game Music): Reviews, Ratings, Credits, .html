Yes, you can overdo it
Mostly instrumental, quite operatic, also very romantic, dramatic and theatrical. Also quite long... 8 hours and 9 minutes... 154 songs... yeah. This lasts more than some artists's entire discographies.

As I think it would be unnecessarily exhausting for me to get into specific parts of this and I perceive it all as one big thing, I'll just say this had moments of drama and troublesome feelings as well as elegance and beauty. Truly an opera piece brought to a recording. Electric guitars, epic 11-minute symphonic metal tunes, ambient moments too, it's really a journey...

Unfortunately this is never not going to weigh on my rating - as an album it's too long. It's probably not as big of a factor if you're experiencing the soundtrack from the video game. But I am looking at it as a music listener. I don't care who you are, an 8 hour long album is a nearly impossible task if you want to make it work, in my opinion. I think a very significant quality of a good album is the ability to be remembered and live in your head. And there is very little from the record here that really stands out in its absolutely mindblowing playtime. If it could have been made into something under an hour, having only its best parts accented, perhaps that could regain 0.5 stars for me.

As a music listener, I would never recommend this to anyone. I'll probably just remember it as "that 8 hour thing". It's too much hastle for something that's not quite the peak of music, even at its best. If you are here from the game that this corresponds to, just play the game, I don't know...
